## The decision came before the repair

The athlete approved the product judgement the diagnosis could not make.
Frozen v0 is the stability floor: on the pre-registered settled S11 battery,
median immediate capacity displacement may not exceed 2.1%, worst may not
exceed 3.3%, and ninety-day residue must remain below 0.5%.

This is a lexicographic gate. A candidate that fails any stability limit is
ineligible for ranking by accuracy, however much its MAE improves. The
evaluation code contains a direct counterexample: a candidate at MAE 0.1604
with 52.2% immediate displacement loses to stable v0 at MAE 0.3152.

The limits freeze previously recorded v0 behaviour. They were not selected
after seeing a repaired estimator and are not claimed to be physiological
constants. They make `PRODUCT.md`'s phrase "barely moves capacity" testable.

## Calibration choice also pre-registered

D21 option 2 was selected before any post-repair calibration output was read:
revise and version the §19.4 bands. The existing bands were derived from the
linear engine and the simulator already misses four of six. Replacement bands
must carry the simulator column and remain Grade C.
