## What this audit found

### The defect: confidence rose on a day the model lost work

**Day 45 of the development window: three of fifteen working sets carried no
effort rating. The engine discarded all three — and reported confidence of
0.9971, higher than either neighbouring day.**

Every link in the chain was individually defensible. A set with no effort
rating cannot have a dose computed, so it produced nothing. Dose quality
weights each set by its surviving contribution, and a set that contributed
nothing has zero weight, so it fell outside the sum. The day therefore looked
— to the confidence system — like a slightly shorter session in which every
set was well recorded.

The result contradicted what `DATA.md` §19.5 says confidence is for. It is an
index of *evidence sufficiency*, and a day that lost a fifth of its session is
a day with less evidence behind it, not more.

It is worth being precise about why this differs from the missed-session case
the same section makes a point of. Not training is genuinely informative about
fatigue: the states decayed and there is nothing left to be uncertain about, so
confidence rightly holds. Training and not recording it is the opposite — the
work happened, the fatigue is real, and the model simply does not know about
it. The two look similar in the data and call for opposite answers.

**Scale before the fix:** 115 sets, 2.1% of dose-bearing work, spread over 95
days — 77 losing one set, 16 losing two, 2 losing three. Imputing at the median
logged effort, the discarded work amounted to roughly **2.2% of total local
fatigue** the model never accounted for. Small here because `DATA.md` §17
generates unrated sets at about 2%; real logs are patchier and the same
mechanism scales with them silently.

### The fix

`MODEL.tex` gains a section, and the engine implements it: a working set with
no effort rating **contributes its full dose at an imputed reserve** rather
than being discarded. The reserve is the median of the athlete's last 30 rated
sets of that exercise, falling back to their recent rated sets generally, and
only then to a prior of 2 — each fallback a step further from the person, so
each taken only when the one before it has too little to say.

The set carries `u_confidence = low`, which propagates into dose quality and
lowers confidence in every state it touches. **Day 45 now reads 0.9269 against
neighbours of 0.9726 and 0.9555.**

It is never eligible for performance estimation. Imputing a dose and inventing
a performance observation are different acts: a guessed effort supports an
estimate of how much fatigue a set caused, and supports nothing at all about
what the athlete was capable of. One-step-ahead residuals are unchanged by the
fix (+0.0124 → +0.0119), which is the evidence that this boundary held.

The trace labels such contributors "(effort not logged)", so a displayed number
resting on guessed effort says so.

### One check whose premise was wrong

The four-channel check originally asked that every working set populate all
four dose channels, and failed on the 115 unrated sets. The engine was right;
the check was asserting a consequence rather than the property it meant.

This is the same failure mode recorded three times in Layer 2's build log. It
is worth noting that here it was *productive*: rescoping the check to
transformable sets is what surfaced the defect above.

## Marginal passes worth watching

- **Capacity error at 8.5% mean absolute** across all 27 exercises, against a
  10% threshold. The six main lifts average 6.0%; the isolation movements pull
  the mean up, which the L2.6 fit-quality component independently detects. The
  threshold is not in danger but it is not comfortable either.
- **rho reaches both clip bounds** (0.800 and 1.150) over the window. The
  inverse-crime audit records that true recovery propensity spans a wider range
  than the engine can express, so clipping is expected — but a channel sitting
  on its limits is worth watching for how often, which L3.2 should measure.
- **Confidence sits in the `high` band on 498 of 520 days.** Defensible on
  clean synthetic data with near-complete wearables, but an index that is
  almost always high is close to being no index at all. The real test is
  L3.12, where the inputs are worse.
- ~~**Local fatigue correlates +0.450 with the simulator's hidden state**~~ —
  **wrong, corrected at L3.1.** This check paired the engine's day-N state,
  captured at the close of day N, with the simulator's day-N state *on waking*,
  which is sixteen hours and one training session earlier. Paired correctly:
  local fatigue **+0.949**, systemic **+0.973**, neuromuscular **+0.956**.
  The accompanying reasoning — that a modest correlation was expected from the
  known misspecification — was a plausible explanation of an artefact, which is
  the more instructive part of the error.
