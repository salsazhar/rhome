## Outcome

The estimator is now admissible under D23, but it is not competitive on the
athlete's historical log.

The coordinated candidate passes the hard robustness gate, preserves or
improves all six frozen synthetic contrast checks, and does not worsen settled
synthetic one-step error. On the 125-row historical sample matched across every
D20 predictor, however, it scores MAE **0.2955** against 0.1255--0.1486 for the
three required recency baselines. This is development evidence under D22, not
validation, and LIM-31 remains open.

Prospective validation begins after this stage's freeze. It must record exact
machine/station/attachment context and set role, compare only sessions
postdating the freeze, and stratify stable contexts from confirmed equipment
changes.

## The repair is one mechanism, not four isolated patches

The implementation follows the interaction exposed by diagnosis:

- cable and machine capacity is keyed by recorded equipment context;
  free-weight and bodyweight-derived capacity remains shared;
- capacity learns once per exercise-context per session from the median of all
  eligible fatigue-corrected set evidence, removing within-session
  pseudo-replication;
- the ordinary session rate is 0.2063, derived from a three-session response
  half-life rather than fitted to the historical log; medium-confidence and
  anchor session rates are 0.1591 and 0.50;
- the Huber bound is scale-aware:
  `1.345 × max(0.10, 1.4826 × MAD)` over the last twenty session
  innovations, with the prior floor held until four innovations exist;
- ordinary and anchor sessions have explicit 2.1% and 3.3% movement caps at
  every maturity, so responsiveness cannot buy fragility;
- local fatigue is curved by observable prior dose-bearing sets for each
  muscle: `1 + 0.25 × min(position, 4)`. The base local scale is 0.06, so
  the saturated per-set impulse is no larger than v0's 0.12. Stimulus,
  neuromuscular strain and systemic load are unchanged.

The source session order is now preserved through synthetic and Spotr
ingestion because position is a model input. The ranking and calibration
evaluators apply the same observable position path, while their independent
audit reproduces it by separate arithmetic.

## Candidate selection used frozen synthetic criteria

The real-log fitted combination (rate 0.30, absolute clip 0.35, curvature near
γ=2) reached MAE 0.1604 but permits a 52.2% one-session movement and is
ineligible under D23. It was not shipped.

After the ranking evaluator's novelty and position bypasses were fixed, γ=1
was also rejected: it overshot the high-volume contrast to 1.606 and the
deload-dose contrast to 1.357. The chosen γ=0.25 candidate is the smallest
maximum-impulse-normalised form that clears every frozen criterion.

| synthetic criterion | frozen v0 | L3.14 |
|---|---:|---:|
| settled one-step MAE | 0.039652 | **0.039581** |
| failure / matched non-failure | 0.576 | 0.576 |
| high / ordinary volume | 0.788 | **1.156** |
| lower compound / arm isolation, systemic | 0.764 | 0.764 |
| heavy compound / isolation, neuromuscular | 0.688 | 0.688 |
| accumulation / deload dose | 0.681 | **0.943** |
| post- / pre-deload performance | 0.564 | **0.678** |

High volume overshoots truth by 15.6%, but that is closer than v0's 21.2%
shortfall. All six directions hold; every affected ratio moves toward 1.0 and
none of the unaffected ratios moves away.

The repair does **not** solve deload attribution. The independent audit now
attributes a deload change to capacity 4.0 times as much as to freshness,
against 3.4 times before, with excess capacity movement
+0.0124 ± 0.0045 over matched ordinary gaps. LIM-23 therefore remains a
separate open defect.
